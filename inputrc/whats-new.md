# What's new in Inputrc

## 1.0.0 (not yet released)

The first release.

- A keyboard-driven editor for iPhone, worked with Ctrl and Meta keys: moving by characters, words,
  lines, sentences and paragraphs; the mark and region; a kill ring for cutting and pasting;
  undo that can itself be undone; counts for any command; and M-x to run any command by its name.
- Incremental search as you type, which is also the quickest way to move about; search and replace,
  with patterns (regular expressions), asking at each match or replacing them all.
- Buffers: several files open at once, each keeping its place, its mark and its undo.
- Questions and file names answered in the line at the bottom, with completion of names and
  commands (TAB), and a list of the choices.
- Its own keyboard with Ctrl, Meta and Esc, and a row of keys that suits the kind of file in view.
- Files edited where they live: Inputrc's own folder, iCloud Drive, or other apps through the Files
  app. Nothing is copied, and unsaved changes are kept as drafts.
- Find and replace across all files, word completion, comments by the file's language, filling and
  indenting, and a Markdown preview.
- Syntax colours chosen by the file name, six colour themes, and six fonts.
- Works with Bluetooth keyboards (Option as Meta) and VoiceOver.
- A 14-day free trial, then a one-off unlock. No subscription, no adverts, and no data collected.
