# Diurnall support

Diurnall is a journal for iPhone: a plain-text note for every day, with an Agenda, tasks, a
calendar, repeating events and reminders built from your own lines.

## Contact

Write to **robertfb-apps@posteo.uk**. The address is the same for all of the developer's apps, so
please say the email is about Diurnall. It helps to include:

- the app's version and build, shown at the foot of Diurnall's menu (the three lines, top right)
  (for example 1.0.0 (81));
- your iPhone model and iOS version;
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.

Please do not send notes you would not want read. Replies come from the same address.

## Questions and answers

### Your notes

**Where are my notes?** One text file a day, named by its date (such as 2026-11-19-Thu.md), in the
notes folder. By default that is Diurnall's own folder: in the Files app, under On My iPhone,
Diurnall. Any text editor can open them.

**Can I keep them somewhere else, such as iCloud Drive or Dropbox?** Yes. In Diurnall's Settings,
Notes folder, choose any folder the Files app can reach, on iCloud Drive or with another provider
that appears in Files. Your notes are then outside the app, and any syncing to your other devices
is done by that provider, not by Diurnall. Diurnall offers to copy the notes already written;
nothing is moved or deleted. A note that a provider keeps online only cannot be searched until it
has downloaded.

**What happens if I delete Diurnall?** If your notes are in Diurnall's own folder, they are deleted
with it. A folder outside the app is not affected: install Diurnall again, choose that folder in
Settings, Notes folder, and everything comes back. Settings, Backup, makes a zip of the whole folder
to keep elsewhere, and Settings reminds you if you have not backed up for a month.

**Does Diurnall sync between devices by itself?** No. Use a folder kept by a service that syncs, such as iCloud Drive, for that.

### Tags, tasks and the Agenda

**How does a line get into the Agenda?** End it with a tag, such as "9am Dentist #appt". The Tag key
above the keyboard adds one. A tag only shows in the Agenda if it is set to, under Settings, Tags.

**How do I make a task?** Tap the [ ] key, or use the + in Tasks, which can also set a due date.
Tick it in the note, in Tasks, or in Preview. A due date is written at the end of the line, as
"due:2026-11-05", and the task then shows on that day.

**Why does a task show as "From" a date?** That is the day it was written in, not a due date.

### Repeating events and reminders

**What are repeating events?** Events that repeat every so many days or weeks, or happen once over
several days, set up from the menu, Repeating events. They show in the Agenda and Calendar and are
never written into your notes, so Search does not find them.

**How do reminders work?** Turn on Remind me in Settings. A line with the Reminder tag and a time
at the start, such as "10am Dentist #rmdr", rings before it; a line with the tag and no time rings
at the time of day set in Settings. Reminders are notifications on your iPhone and need its
permission; if the iPhone's Settings app has Diurnall's notifications or sounds off, they will be
silent.

### Trial and unlock

**What does the trial include?** Every feature, for 14 days. After the trial, you can still read,
search, copy, share and back up all your notes. Writing and changing them need the unlock, a single
purchase, not a subscription. Restore purchases is on the purchase screen and in Settings.
