# Diurnall privacy policy

Last changed: 19 November 2026

Diurnall is a journal for iPhone, made by Robert Frost-Bridges, an individual developer in the
United Kingdom. This page says what happens to your information when you use it.

## In short

Diurnall collects no data. It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no
tracking. Diurnall itself never sends your notes, your tags or anything about you to the developer
or to anyone else.

## Your notes

Your notes stay where you keep them. In Diurnall's own folder they are on your iPhone (in the Files
app, under On My iPhone, Diurnall). If you choose a folder on iCloud Drive or elsewhere, your notes
are kept there, and any syncing is done by that service, not by Diurnall. Deleting Diurnall deletes
its own folder and its settings; a folder you chose elsewhere is not touched.

The notes folder also holds the tags file, repeating events, the template and the Scratch page, all
plain files. **Back up** makes a zip of the folder on your iPhone and offers it to the iPhone's share
sheet; where it goes is your choice, and Diurnall does not see it again.

## Reminders

Reminders are notifications made and kept on your iPhone. Diurnall asks the iPhone's permission when
you turn them on. Nothing about them is sent to the developer or to Apple by Diurnall.

## Purchases

The free trial and the unlock are in-app purchases made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles
the payment and your Apple Account. Diurnall is told only whether the trial or the unlock belongs
to your Apple Account, and the developer never sees your payment details. Apple's own privacy
policy covers what Apple does: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.

## Information Apple may share

If you have chosen, in your iPhone's settings, to share analytics with app developers, Apple may
give the developer crash reports and anonymous usage figures for Diurnall. This is Apple's choice
to offer and yours to make; Diurnall adds nothing to it. You can change it in the iPhone's Settings
app (not Diurnall's): Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

People testing Diurnall through Apple's TestFlight may send feedback through TestFlight. Apple then
passes the feedback, with the tester's email address, to the developer.

## If you write to us

If you email robertfb-apps@posteo.uk (the address for all of the developer's apps), your address and
message are used only to answer you and to fix what you report. They are not shared or used for
anything else, and are deleted when they are no longer needed for that. The mail is held by Posteo,
an email provider in Germany. Please say the email is about Diurnall.

## Children

Diurnall collects nothing from anyone, children included.

## Your rights

You can ask what information the developer holds about you (normally none, or only your emails), and
ask for it to be corrected or deleted, by writing to robertfb-apps@posteo.uk. In the United Kingdom
you may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: <https://ico.org.uk/>.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date. Diurnall will not
start collecting data without saying so here first.
