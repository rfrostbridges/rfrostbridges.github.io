# Diurnall

A journal for iPhone: a plain-text note for every day, with an Agenda, tasks, a calendar, repeating
events and reminders built from your own lines. Your notes are ordinary files that you own. A
one-off unlock after a free trial. No subscription, no advertising, no data collected.
