# What's new in Diurnall

## 1.0.0 (not yet released)

The first release.

- A note for every day, saved as you type and kept as a plain text (Markdown) file you can open in
  any editor, with a template for new days and a Scratch page for loose scraps.
- Tags: end a line with one, such as "9am Dentist #appt", and it shows in the Agenda on its day.
- Tasks with tick boxes, due dates and an Urgent mark; a list of every task; tasks due on a day
  shown at the top of that day's note.
- An Agenda looking ahead and back with a filter by time and tag, a Calendar with a list of each
  day's entries and a Go to, and Search across every note.
- Repeating events (every so many days or weeks, or once over several days) that show without
  changing your notes.
- Reminders that ring before a time you write, or at a time of day you choose for lines with no
  time; five sounds, all on your iPhone.
- A key row for writing: Undo, Preview, Tag, a task box, the date and time, and a grid of headings,
  bold, italic, links, lists and more.
- Your folder: Diurnall's own, or one on iCloud Drive, with a one-button zip backup.
- Fonts, sizes, line spacing, light or dark and one colour for the app; VoiceOver.
- A 14-day free trial, then a one-off unlock. No subscription, no adverts, and no data collected.
