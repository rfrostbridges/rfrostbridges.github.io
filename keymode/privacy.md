# Keymode privacy policy

Last changed: 19 October 2026

Keymode (on the App Store as "Keymode: Modal Text Editor") is a text editor for iPhone, made by
Robert Frost-Bridges, an individual developer in the United Kingdom. This page says what
happens to your information when you use it.

## In short

Keymode collects no data. It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no
tracking. Keymode itself never sends your text, your file names or anything about you to the
developer or to anyone else.

## Your files

Your files stay where you keep them. Files in Keymode's own folder are on your iPhone (in the
Files app, under On My iPhone, Keymode). A file you open from elsewhere, such as iCloud Drive or
another app's folder, is edited where it lives and is not copied. If that place is synced, for
example by iCloud, the syncing is done by that service, not by Keymode.

Unsaved text is kept on your iPhone as a draft, so that it is not lost if Keymode is closed. Your
settings are also kept on your iPhone. Deleting Keymode deletes these, and the files in Keymode's
own folder.

Keymode reads the iPhone clipboard only when you ask it to paste.

## Purchases

The free trial and the unlock are in-app purchases made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles
the payment and your Apple Account. Keymode is told only whether the trial or the unlock belongs
to your Apple Account, and the developer never sees your payment details. Apple's own privacy
policy covers what Apple does: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.

## Information Apple may share

If you have chosen, in your iPhone's settings, to share analytics with app developers, Apple may
give the developer crash reports and anonymous usage figures for Keymode. This is Apple's choice
to offer and yours to make; Keymode adds nothing to it. You can change it in Settings, Privacy &
Security, Analytics & Improvements.

People testing Keymode through Apple's TestFlight may send feedback through TestFlight. Apple then
passes the feedback, with the tester's email address, to the developer.

## If you write to us

If you email keymode@posteo.uk, your address and message are used only to answer you and to
fix what you report. They are not shared or used for anything else, and are deleted when they
are no longer needed for that. The mail is held by Posteo, an email provider in Germany.

The **Get help or report a problem** button in Keymode's Help only opens a new email in your own
mail app. It fills in Keymode's version, the iOS version and which keyboard setting is in use,
and nothing else: no text or names from your files. You see the whole email, can change or delete
any of it, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

## Children

Keymode collects nothing from anyone, children included.

## Your rights

You can ask what information the developer holds about you (normally none, or only your emails),
and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, by writing to keymode@posteo.uk. In the United
Kingdom you may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: <https://ico.org.uk/>.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date. Keymode will not
start collecting data without saying so here first.
