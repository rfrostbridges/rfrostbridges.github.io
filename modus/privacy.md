# Modus privacy policy

Last changed: 16 October 2026

Modus (on the App Store as "Modus: Vim-Style Editor") is a text editor for iPhone, made by
Robert Frost-Bridges, an individual developer in the United Kingdom. This page says what
happens to your information when you use it.

## In short

Modus collects no data. It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no
tracking. Modus itself never sends your text, your file names or anything about you to the
developer or to anyone else.

## Your files

Your files stay where you keep them. Files in Modus's own folder are on your iPhone (in the
Files app, under On My iPhone, Modus). A file you open from elsewhere, such as iCloud Drive or
another app's folder, is edited where it lives and is not copied. If that place is synced, for
example by iCloud, the syncing is done by that service, not by Modus.

Unsaved text is kept on your iPhone as a draft, so that it is not lost if Modus is closed. Your
settings are also kept on your iPhone. Deleting Modus deletes these, and the files in Modus's
own folder.

Modus reads the iPhone clipboard only when you ask it to paste.

## Purchases

The free trial and the unlock are in-app purchases made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles
the payment and your Apple Account. Modus is told only whether the trial or the unlock belongs
to your Apple Account, and the developer never sees your payment details. Apple's own privacy
policy covers what Apple does: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.

## Information Apple may share

If you have chosen, in your iPhone's settings, to share analytics with app developers, Apple may
give the developer crash reports and anonymous usage figures for Modus. This is Apple's choice
to offer and yours to make; Modus adds nothing to it. You can change it in Settings, Privacy &
Security, Analytics & Improvements.

People testing Modus through Apple's TestFlight may send feedback through TestFlight. Apple then
passes the feedback, with the tester's email address, to the developer.

## If you write to us

If you email modus_app@posteo.net, your address and message are used only to answer you and to
fix what you report. They are not shared or used for anything else, and are deleted when they
are no longer needed for that. The mail is held by Posteo, an email provider in Germany.

## Children

Modus collects nothing from anyone, children included.

## Your rights

You can ask what information the developer holds about you (normally none, or only your emails),
and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, by writing to modus_app@posteo.net. In the United
Kingdom you may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: <https://ico.org.uk/>.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date. Modus will not
start collecting data without saying so here first.
